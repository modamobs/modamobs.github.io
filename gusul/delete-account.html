Delete your account — Marble Land Grab

Developer: Smart it (modamobs) · 한국어

Delete in the app

  1. Open Marble Land Grab.
  2. Tap Settings at the bottom of the main screen.
  3. Tap Delete account → Delete.

Deletion is immediate.

If you already uninstalled the app

Email yamoda24@gmail.com with one of the following so we can find your account:

  • The email address of the Google account you linked (if you linked one)
  • The name shown on the leaderboard and roughly when you last played (if you did not)

We delete the data and reply within 7 days of your request.

What is deleted

  • Your game records backed up on the server (stats, achievements, Daily Challenge records, name, marble)
  • Your leaderboard entries (name, marble, scores)
  • Your account and Google link (email address, account identifier)

What stays

  • Game records on your phone — removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics) — not linked to you and deleted automatically after 14 months.

See the privacy policy for details.
