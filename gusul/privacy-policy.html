Privacy Policy — Marble Land Grab

Last updated: 2026-09-17 · 한국어

Summary

You can play without an account, and your game records stay on your device. The developer does not collect personal information such as your name or contact details. The app does send anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics so the game can be improved. This version has no ads and no in-app purchases, and the game does not need an internet connection.

1. What the app stores

The following is kept only in your device's storage. Nobody, including the developer, can read it from outside, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

  • The display name you type
  • Your chosen mode, obstacle setting, language and sound on/off
  • Total rounds, wins, best record, achievements and unlocked marbles
  • Daily Challenge scores and recent results

2. Internet connection

The app does not connect to the internet to play. Every mode against bots is computed on your device.

The browser version at modamobs.github.io also plays against bots only. A separate online mode exists on the development server; when it is used, your display name and marble position are relayed in real time to the other players in the same room. That information is not stored and disappears when you leave the room. Connection times and IP addresses may remain briefly in the hosting provider's server logs.

3. Usage statistics (Google Analytics)

To see which modes people play and where they stop, the app sends anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics 4. What is sent are numbers: rounds started and finished, the mode and upgrades you chose, your rank and how much land you took. In doing so Google also processes a device identifier and an approximate location (country level) derived from your IP address.

The display name you type is never sent. No phone number, email, contacts or photos are sent either. The statistics are kept in Google Analytics for 14 months and then deleted.

4. Ads

This version has no ads and does not use the Advertising ID. If ads are added later, this document will be updated first, along with the Data Safety section of the store listing.

5. In-app purchases

This version has no in-app purchases.

6. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell any information. Apart from using Google (Google LLC) to process the usage statistics described above, nothing is shared with third parties.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes every record stored on the device. Nothing is kept on the developer's servers, so there is no separate deletion request to make.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this document is updated.
