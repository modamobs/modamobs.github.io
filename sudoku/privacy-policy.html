본 게임은 로그인이나 회원가입을 요구하지 않습니다. 개발자는 이용자를 식별할 수 있는 어떤 정보도 전달받지 않습니다. 이름, 이메일, 주소, 전화번호, 생년월일, 결제 수단 정보를 수집하지 않으며 위치·연락처·사진·카메라·마이크에 접근하지 않습니다.

2. 기기 안에만 저장되는 정보

아래 정보는 기기 내부 저장소에만 남고 외부로 전송되지 않습니다. 개발자를 포함한 누구도 볼 수 없으며, 앱을 삭제하면 함께 삭제됩니다. 설정의 "데이터 초기화"로도 지울 수 있습니다.

  • 진행 중인 판, 푼 판 수, 난이도별 기록, 데일리 챌린지 완료 날짜

본 게임은 Google AdMob을 통해 배너·전면·보상형 광고를 표시합니다. 광고를 위해 Google이 아래 정보를 수집·처리할 수 있습니다. 개발자는 이 정보를 받지 않습니다.

Classic Sudoku Privacy Policy

Effective: September 5, 2026

Classic Sudoku (package com.mobs.sudoku, "the Game") requires no account. Your progress is stored only on your device. Google AdMob collects some information to show ads in the Game.

1. Information the developer collects

The Game has no sign-in and no sign-up. The developer receives no information that could identify you. We do not collect names, e-mail addresses, postal addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth or payment details, and we do not access location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays in the device's local storage and is never sent anywhere. It is removed when you uninstall the Game, or with "Reset data" in Settings.

  • The puzzle in progress, solved counts, per-difficulty records, completed Daily Challenge dates
  • In-game coins and hints
  • Sound, vibration, theme and language settings

3. Information collected by ads (Google AdMob)

The Game shows banner, interstitial and rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process the following. The developer does not receive it.

DataPurpose
Device identifiers such as the Advertising ID and App Set IDAd serving, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Approximate location inferred from IP addressRegional ads, analytics
App interactions: launches, taps, ad viewsAdvertising, analytics
Diagnostics: device model, OS version, app performanceAnalytics

See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Google → Ads). Users in the EEA and the UK see a consent dialog on first launch and can change their choice in Settings at any time.

4. Children

The Game is rated for everyone and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Security and retention

The developer keeps no user data on any server, so there is no server-side data to be breached. Communication with AdMob is encrypted (TLS).

6. Changes

When this policy changes, this page is updated together with the effective date.
