Rocket Escape Account Deletion

App: Rocket Escape (com.mobs.rocketescape) · Developer: Smart it

Delete in the Game

  1. Open the Rocket Escape app or the web version.
  2. Go to the 설정 (Settings) tab.
  3. Under 계정 (Account), tap 계정 삭제 (Delete account) and confirm.

Your data is deleted from the server immediately and cannot be recovered. The button is disabled during a flight.

Request by e-mail

If you cannot use the app, e-mail yamoda24@gmail.com with the subject "Rocket Escape account deletion". If you linked a Google account, send it from that address; otherwise include the name shown on the leaderboard. We will delete the account within 30 days and reply.
