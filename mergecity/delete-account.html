Merge City 2048 — Account & Data Deletion

App: Merge City 2048 (com.mobs.civilization2048) · Last updated: 2 September 2026

Merge City 2048 can be played without signing in. An account exists only if you turned on Cloud save in settings and signed in with a Google account. You can delete it at any time using either method below.

Delete in the app

  1. Open the game.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Under Cloud save, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete. It takes effect immediately.

If you cannot use the app

If you lost the device or cannot install the app, email us from the Google account address you signed in with, so we can verify the request.

Email: yamoda24@gmail.com
Please put "Delete account" in the subject. We act on it and reply within 30 days of confirming the request.

What is deleted

DeletedKept
Your sign-in account (the name and email address held by Google Firebase Authentication)
The progress stored on the server: city rebuilding progress, material in stock, building codex, best score, the run in progress and booster counts 		The progress saved on this device. Deleting the account removes only the server copy, so the game on your device carries on. To clear the device too, uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset data.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Copies left in backups are removed within 30 days. No personal data is retained after deletion.
