과일 콕콕(패키지 com.mobs.fruitgame, 토스 미니앱 fruit-kokkok, 이하 "본 게임")은 가입이나 로그인 없이 바로 시작합니다. 결제, 채팅, 사용 통계·오류 보고 도구는 없습니다. "랭킹"을 위해 닉네임과 점수를 Supabase에 저장하고, 안드로이드 앱의 광고는 Google AdMob이 처리합니다.

5. 토스 미니앱·원스토어·웹 버전

토스 앱 안의 미니앱, 원스토어 앱에서 여는 버전, modamobs.github.io의 웹 버전에는 현재 광고와 결제가 없습니다. 랭킹은 1항과 같이 동작합니다. 토스·원스토어 계정 정보는 본 게임이 받지 않습니다. 화면 글꼴과 이모지 글꼴을 Google Fonts(fonts.googleapis.com)와 jsDelivr(cdn.jsdelivr.net)에서 받고, 원스토어 버전은 원스토어의 게임 SDK(h5sdk.onestore.net)를 불러옵니다. 웹 페이지는 GitHub Pages가 제공합니다. 이 과정에서 각 회사는 일반적인 웹 접속처럼 IP 주소와 브라우저 정보를 받을 수 있습니다.

Fruit Kok Kok Privacy Policy

Effective: October 27, 2026

Fruit Kok Kok (과일 콕콕, package com.mobs.fruitgame, Toss mini app fruit-kokkok, "the Game") needs no sign-up or login. It has no payments, no chat and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. For the leaderboard it stores a nickname and score on Supabase; ads in the Android app are served by Google AdMob.

1. Data the developer collects (leaderboard)

When a game ends with a score above zero, the Game sends the data below. A random nickname (for example "새콤한딸기0420") is set on first launch and can be changed on the ranking screen at any time.

DataDescriptionPurpose
Player IDA random identifier (UUID) created on your device, not linked to your name or device detailsGroup your records and show your rank
NicknameA random or self-chosen name of 2–10 characters (Korean, English letters, digits)Shown on the leaderboard
Score and recordScore, fruits cleared, boards cleared, time recordedRanking and filtering out impossible scores

This data is sent over an encrypted connection (TLS) to and stored in a database hosted by Supabase, Inc. Only the nickname and score are public on the leaderboard; the player ID is never shown. Please do not use personal information such as your real name or contact details as a nickname. We do not collect your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data.

2. Retention and deletion

Leaderboard records are kept while the leaderboard is operated. To have a record or nickname deleted, e-mail us with the nickname; we will delete it promptly.

3. Data stored only on your device

The sound setting, your best score, your nickname and player ID, and any score not yet sent (offline) are stored only on the device (browser storage for the web version). They are deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the site data.

4. Data collected by ads (Google AdMob, Android app)

The Android app may show a Google AdMob interstitial ad when you leave the result screen after a game (at most once every two games). For ads, Google may collect and process the following. The developer does not receive this data.

DataPurpose
Device identifiers such as the Advertising ID and App Set IDAd serving, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Approximate location inferred from IP addressRegional ads, analytics
App interactions such as launches and ad impressions/viewsAdvertising, analytics
Diagnostics such as device model, OS version and app performanceAnalytics

Users in the EEA and UK may see Google's consent form before ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete the Advertising ID in device settings. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS).

5. Toss mini app, OneStore and web versions

The mini app inside the Toss app, the version opened from the OneStore app and the web version on modamobs.github.io currently have no ads or payments; the leaderboard works as in section 1. The Game does not receive your Toss or OneStore account information. Fonts and the emoji font are loaded from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) and jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), the OneStore version loads OneStore's game SDK (h5sdk.onestore.net), and web pages are served by GitHub Pages; like any web request, these companies may receive your IP address and browser information.

6. App permissions

The Android app uses only internet/network state, plus the Advertising ID and Android Ad Services (Privacy Sandbox) permissions added by the ads SDK. It does not request location, contacts, photos/files, camera or microphone.

7. Children

The Game is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and its effective date.
